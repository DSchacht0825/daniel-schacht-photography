Questions

The things people actually worry about.

I hate having my picture taken. Is this going to be awkward?

That describes most of my clients. I direct every detail, keep the session conversational, and show you shots on the back of the camera as we go. Most people relax within ten minutes, and many of them say afterward that it was actually fun.

What should I wear?

Wear what you'd wear on a day you feel great. Solid colors work best and textures look good on camera. Bring options and we'll choose together. After you book, I'll send a short guide.

Will I still look like me after retouching?

Yes. I clean up stray hairs, blemishes, and distractions without reshaping your face. People who meet you in person should recognize you right away.

How long until I get my photos?

Your gallery arrives in 3–5 business days. If you're up against a deadline, rush 24-hour delivery is available.

Can you photograph my whole team?

Yes. I bring the lighting to your office anywhere in San Diego County and set it up so everyone matches. Text me for a group quote.

How do I pay?

Payment is due at the session. I accept cards, Venmo, Zelle, and cash.
