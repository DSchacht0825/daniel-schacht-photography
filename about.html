About Daniel

You're not hiring a camera. You're hiring the person behind it.

Lots of people own good gear. The difference is whether the person holding it makes you feel safe enough to be yourself.

Hi, I'm Daniel

I photograph people. Not poses.

I've spent years photographing people in every setting: executives in studio light, musicians under stage lights, families on the beach, and folks in my community on some of their biggest days.

What I've learned is that almost nobody feels comfortable in front of a camera. Executives tense up just like everyone else. So I don't start with the camera. I start by getting to know you: we talk, we laugh at how weird this is, and I tell you exactly what to do so you don't have to think about it.

By the time we're ten minutes in, most people have forgotten they're being photographed. Those are the moments I'm waiting for.

"Everyone has a version of themselves they wish others could see. My job is to find it and photograph it."
Daniel Schacht

What you can count on

What I promise every person who books with me.

i.

You'll never feel lost

I direct everything: chin, shoulders, hands, and where to look. You never have to guess or "just act natural."

ii.

You'll still look like you

I retouch to clean things up, not to change your face. People who meet you later should recognize you right away.

iii.

You'll hear back fast

Text me and I'll usually reply the same day. Your finished gallery arrives in 3–5 days, and rush delivery is available.

Trusted by

Authors, magazines, and real people.

I was the featured photographer for Robert Callahan's book Fire in the Hole and for Roast Magazine. Most of my work, though, is for business owners, coaches, pastors, nonprofit leaders, artists, and graduates around San Diego County.
